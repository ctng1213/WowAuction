# Plan: Claude's plans (the first write tool, CP0–CP5)

Written for: the user, who approves it (the mockup `docs/mockups/claude-plans.html`, and §1's three "planner's call" items), and the Opus session that runs the batch. Drafted 2026-11-09 from `docs/plans/mcp-server.md` §11 (the user's decisions); revised 2026-11-10 with the user's answers to the draft's open questions (§1, items 6–14). Tasks are tagged **Opus** (judgement) or **Sonnet** (routine), each with a one-line reason. **Status: approved by the user 2026-11-10** (the plan, the mockup and §1's planner's calls, including the two edge cases in §1).

**What this batch is.** Claude may put its own trades into your plan. A pick is a plan action like the engine's, labelled **Claude**, that the addon shows and tracks the same way. Four kinds: post your stock, flip (buy, then post), craft (and post the product), and hold (buy, then post at a target price when the hold ends). The engine plans around it: Claude's gold, risk and market room are taken first, and the engine fills only what is left. A pick goes straight in and comes out with Skip; the engine then re-plans to fill the hole, and Claude may add it again. A read tool shows Claude how its picks did. This is the MCP server's first tool that changes state, so it gets its own switch, its own bounds, SEC scenarios, `/security-review` and an independent review.

## 1. What is decided (do not re-litigate)

The user, 2026-11-09 (`docs/plans/mcp-server.md` §11):

1. **One pipeline, a new origin.** Claude's actions are plan actions with `origin = 'claude'`; the engine's are `'engine'`. `actions.source` keeps meaning a buy's vendor or AH, so origin is its own column. Picks live in their own table, because the engine rebuilds the plan at every refresh and re-plan; each rebuild merges them back in until they are done, expired or removed. They reach the addon through `PortfolioPlan.lua` like every action, and the addon and the dashboard label them.
2. **Claude first; the engine plans around it.** The engine treats Claude's actions as already in the plan: their gold, risk and market capacity are taken first, and it fills only the room left. With no room, it adds nothing. It plans no action on an item a Claude pick names.
3. **Straight in, removable.** No approval step. Skip takes a pick out, and the engine re-plans to fill the hole.
4. **Tracking comes free.** The addon's capture and AHSync's matching of events to actions track any plan action. The batch adds totals per origin ("Claude's picks: 6 done, +1g 20s realized") and a read tool for Claude.
5. **To design** (this plan): the write tool's checks, the labels (mockup first), and the security review of the first tool that writes.

The user, 2026-11-10 (answers to the draft's open questions):

6. **A second switch for writes.** "Let Claude add picks to my plan", `mcp_writes` in `config.toml`, off by default, `POST /api/mcp {"writes": true|false}` with the dashboard's token checks. Enable alone stays read-only, as every earlier Enable press agreed to. With writes off, the write tools answer a tool error naming the switch; the read tools and `list_picks` still work; picks already in the plan stay until they end.
7. **Skip is the remove.** The dashboard's Skip and the addon's "Mark as skipped" on any of a pick's actions remove the pick; `remove_pick` is Claude's way. No new button.
8. **A pick may exceed your risk tolerance.** `add_pick` never refuses for the risk budget. Its answer warns plainly, with the numbers: how much of the risk room the pick takes and that the engine then has nothing left to plan. This is §11.2 as written: the engine's capacity is `max(0, limit − committed)`, a pick's reservation is part of `committed`, so "with no room, it adds nothing" happens by the existing arithmetic. The gold-in-hand check stays: you can't buy with gold you don't have.
9. **The count cap** is `claude_picks_max`, default 20 active picks per market, allowed 1–50.
10. **The expiry** is `expires_days`, default 7, allowed 1–30, counted from when the pick is added. A hold's expiry must be after its hold.
11. **All four kinds in this batch: `post`, `flip`, `craft` and `hold`.** For a hold, Claude gives the target sell price and the hold in days, so the engine never prices the sale. For a craft, Claude names the recipe, the crafter, the quantity and the sell price.
12. **One active pick per item and market**, counting only `active` picks: a second `add_pick` on an item with an active pick returns that pick with `"already": true` and changes nothing. A removed, expired or done pick never blocks a new one; the new pick gets a new id, and the old one keeps its status and history. Without this a retried call could double a trade.
13. **The user can take a pick out and have Claude add it again.** A dashboard Skip takes effect at once (it queues the re-plan). An addon skip reaches AHSync only at the next SavedVariables read (`/reload` or logout); until then `add_pick` on that item returns the still-active old pick, and its answer says how to clear it: "it is still in your plan; skip it on the dashboard, or /reload, then ask again".
14. **The Skip gap for engine actions** (§4 contradiction 1) is a separate small batch; CP0 records it in LESSONS and marks the SPEC sentence.

Planner's calls, the user to confirm at approval (not asked separately):

15. **Your rules beat Claude's picks.** A pick on an item with rule `ignore` or `no_buy` (the product, for a craft) is refused, "you blacklisted it"; an item flagged "not selling" (§3.3.3) is allowed and the answer says so.
16. **A craft pick's mats are sourced by the engine, not priced by Claude, and a craft that loses gold on its own mats at Claude's price is refused** (§3.1 check 7). A per-mat ceiling would be one more argument per reagent, cover only the AH-bought part, and still need the engine to choose between bags, vendor and AH; the engine's sourcing (`craft.craft_option`, `engine/craft.py:339`: your mats first, then the vendor, then the AH cheapest first) is what it does for its own crafts, deterministic, and the answer lists every mat with its source and price so Claude can remove the pick if it dislikes one. A craft whose mats cost more than its product sells for at Claude's own price is a mistake the data shows, not a market opinion: Claude raises the price or lowers the count.
17. **Mats are not closed to the engine; the product is.** "No engine action on an item a pick names" covers the item Claude trades (a post's or flip's item, a hold's item, a craft's product). A craft's mats stay open to the engine, but the pick's units come first: they are taken out of your stock and off the AH book before the engine generates, so the engine's own crafts and flips of that mat plan with what is left. Closing a mat like Mageweave Cloth to the engine because one pick crafts two Bolts would silence most of the plan.

Inherited from the MCP batch (its §1): the server runs inside AHSync, on 127.0.0.1 only, only while enabled; the key, `Host` and `Origin` checks (SEC19); the bridge answers `tools/list` itself; every tool result is bounded and every error is data (MCP13); standard library only.

## 2. Working rules

- CLAUDE.md's batch rules: one worktree and PR per milestone, scenario rows before code, docs in the same commit, `tools/verify.ps1` per milestone, PRs target `main` (never stacked, mcp-server.md §1.14).
- `ahsync/engine/` stays pure: the merge, the checks' arithmetic and the lifecycle rules are pure functions in a new `engine/picks.py`; reads and writes stay in `state/`, `recommend/` and `mcp/`. Every displayed number (a pick's expected net, chance, the per-origin totals) is engine-made (SPEC §8.5).
- **No ported module changes.** Claude first is done in `recommend/portfolio.py`'s accounting and `engine/week.prepare` (neither is ported); a craft pick's mats are sourced by calling `craft.craft_option` and consumed with `craft_plan.consume` (neither call changes the module), so `engine/craft.py`, `engine/selection.py` and their Lua twins stay identical, and the addon's live reselection needs no change: it already spends within the snapshot's remaining capacity, which the picks have lowered. `craft_plan.py` and `candidates.py` are not ported; `chain_actions` may gain an optional argument for a given post price (§3.3, §9).
- **Determinism** (SPEC §8.6): picks are merged in pick-id order; a pick's action ids derive from its id; `now_ms` is passed in. **The whole-plan snapshot moves exactly once**, in CP1, by the new `origin` column (`'engine'` on every row) and the plan file's new 16th field; every later milestone keeps it byte-identical (the fixture world has no picks). Every golden stays; CP4 adds a new golden (UI26) and changes none.
- Nothing in this batch reads history or runs a simulation outside the full refresh (SPEC §3.1.2): a tool call validates against the published intelligence and the last state snapshot, and the re-plan it asks for is the existing incremental one.
- Size and docstring rules (CLAUDE.md): `web/server.py` and `mcp/tools.py` are near their limits; the pick tools go in a new `mcp/picks.py` (the checks in `mcp/pick_checks.py` if it passes 1,000 lines), the settings endpoint's new field in `web/settings.py`.
- **Security**: the pick tools are a trust boundary (a model writes to the plan). `/security-review` and an independent `code-reviewer` run on CP3a, CP3b and CP4 before merge, with the SEC scenarios, the spec and the diff (not the implementer's transcript).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 The tools (`ahsync/mcp/picks.py`; SPEC §3.8.7, new)

| Tool | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `add_pick` | `market`; `character`; `kind` (`post`, `flip`, `craft`, `hold`); `item` (key, or exact name; for a craft the product, or `recipe` as a spell id); `qty` (units; for a craft, crafts); prices in copper each: a post `price`; a flip `max_buy_price` and `sell_price`; a craft `sell_price`; a hold `max_buy_price`, `target_price` and `hold_days` (1–29); optional `duration_h` (2, 8 or 24; default 24), `expires_days` (1–30; default 7; a hold's must exceed `hold_days`), `note` (≤ 200 characters, shown on the row) | the pick as `list_picks` shows it, every action with its id, and `"replan": "queued"`; a craft lists each mat with its source (whose bags, vendor, AH) and price; `warnings`: the risk room ("this pick takes 6g 40s + deposit of the 6g 49s 40c of risk room left: the engine has nothing left to plan until something sells"), thin data, "not selling". On a duplicate (decision 12): the existing pick, `"already": true` and the clearing text of decision 13. Any failed check: a tool error with one plain sentence naming the check and the number it compared against |
| `remove_pick` | `pick_id` | the pick, status `removed`, `"replan": "queued"`; a pick already closed is returned unchanged with its status |
| `list_picks` | `market`, optional `status` (`active`, `done`, `expired`, `removed`, `all`; default `active`), `limit` (1–200, default 50) | `picks`: each with id, kind, item (and recipe), character, qty, prices, hold, created, expires, status and its reason, its actions (id, kind, item, qty, status, done qty, done at), the engine's expected net and chance at Claude's price, and what it realized so far (`tracking.judge`'s result for its carrier, the same text History shows); `totals`: per status, the count, expected and realized sums |

The `initialize` context gains three facts (MCP7 stays true: facts, no procedure): "a pick is in the plan the moment `add_pick` returns; the user acts on it in game"; "a pick's gold is reserved ahead of the engine, so a large pick leaves the engine nothing to plan"; "the user removes a pick with Skip; a skip made in the addon reaches AHSync at the next /reload". `describe_data` and the `query` allowlist gain `claude_picks` (read-only, like every table there).

**The checks, in order** (each a tool error with the compared value; CP3 scenarios):

1. Writes allowed (decision 6): `mcp_writes` on, else "Claude may read your data but not change your plan: turn on 'Let Claude add picks to my plan' on AHSync's Status page".
2. `market` is configured; `character` exists on it (`characters.market`).
3. The item or recipe: `item` names one item in `items` (a name must match exactly one; the error lists up to 5 near matches by prefix). For a craft: `recipe` is a `recipes.spell_id` with a `product_key`, or `item` is a product that exactly one recipe the crafter knows makes (else the error lists them by spell id); the crafter knows it (`known_recipes`, `db/schema.sql:295`), and the crafter's skill is at least its `skill_req` (`professions.skill`, `schema.sql:279`; "Brugan's Enchanting is 95, the recipe needs 100").
4. Whole copper and units: `qty` an integer 1–10,000 (a craft 1–200 crafts, `craft.MAX_CRAFTS`); every price an integer ≥ 1; `duration_h` in `fees.DURATIONS_H` (`engine/fees.py:11`: 2, 8, 24); `expires_days` 1–30; `hold_days` 1–29 and below `expires_days`; `note` ≤ 200 characters, control characters refused.
5. Your rules win (decision 15): no `ignore` or `no_buy` rule on the item (a craft's product).
6. Sane prices, against the published intelligence, with the engine's own suspect band (FM5): a sell or target price below 10% of `p10_30` or above 10 × `p90_30` is refused, and so is a buy ceiling; a flip's or hold's sale must cover its buy after the cut, `sell_price × (1 − AH_CUT) > max_buy_price`, else "sells for less than it costs". An item with no 30-day percentiles skips the band and the answer says "thin data: the chance is the default".
7. Per character, what the pick needs:
   - a post: the character holds at least `qty` of the item in bags and bank at the last snapshot ("Amaya holds 12 Linen Cloth, not 20");
   - a flip or hold: the character's gold in hand ≥ `qty × max_buy_price` + the post's deposit;
   - a craft: the engine sources `qty` crafts' mats (`craft.craft_option` with `max_crafts = qty` and the product valued at Claude's price after the cut): every mat found (your bags and bank across characters, the vendor, the AH book at the last refresh), else "no Silver Bar to buy: none on the AH and nobody holds one"; the crafter's gold in hand ≥ the mats bought + the deposit; and each craft pays at Claude's price (`option.crafts == qty`), else "at 60s each craft loses 17s 25c on its mats (mats 74s 25c a craft)" (decision 16).
8. The count cap (decision 9): fewer than `claude_picks_max` active picks on the market.
9. One per item (decision 12): no active pick on the item (a craft's product). The answer carries the clearing text of decision 13.

Then the warnings (never a refusal): the risk room (decision 8), `accounting`'s remaining capacity less the other active picks' gold needed, against the pick's gold needed; thin data; "not selling".

**Gold needed** of a pick, the same rule as the engine's rows (SPEC §3.4D.1): a post, its deposit (`fees.deposit` at Claude's price and duration); a flip or hold, `qty × max_buy_price` plus the post's deposit; a craft, the mats bought (`option.mat_gold`, vendor and AH) plus the product's deposit; owned mats cost nothing, as for the engine's crafts. Once part of it is done, the undone share only.

### 3.2 Storage and the write path

- **`claude_picks`** (schema migration: the next free number; `sold-price.md` SP1 takes 30 if it lands first): `pick_id TEXT PK` (`claude:<market>:<created_ms>:<n>`), `market`, `char_id`, `kind`, `key` (the item; a craft's product), `spell_id` (a craft; NULL otherwise), `qty`, `price` (the sell or target price), `max_price` (a flip's or hold's ceiling; NULL otherwise), `hold_days`, `duration_h`, `note`, `created_ms`, `expires_ms`, `status` (`active` | `done` | `expired` | `removed`), `closed_ms`, `reason` (why it closed: `done`, `expired`, `skipped`, `removed by Claude`, `window ended`). Kept 90 days after closing, pruned with actions.
- **`actions.origin TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'engine'`** and the same on `plan_new` (positional insert: the column goes last on both, after `cand_id`). The migration adds it to both; nothing else changes.
- **A pick's actions** carry stable ids and one chain `claude:<pick_id>`, all with `origin = 'claude'` and `cand_id = claude:<key>:<char_id>:<pick_id>` (strategy first, key second, as `positions_pass._outlook` reads it, `recommend/positions_pass.py:156`), so a matched purchase or craft opens a position like a funded opportunity's (`state/positions.py:128-130`), and History, the Portfolio tab's Positions and the committed capital follow with no new code:
  - post: `<pick>:post`;
  - flip: `<pick>:buy` (source `ah`, the chain's carrier) and `<pick>:post`, `parent_id` the buy, dated right after it;
  - hold: the same two, the post dated at the hold's end (the engine's buy-for-later shape, `candidates._later_actions`, `engine/candidates.py:641-663`): `at_ms` = created + `hold_days`, window from there to `expires_ms`, at Claude's target price;
  - craft: the engine's craft chain shape (`craft_plan.chain_actions`, `engine/craft_plan.py:220-275`): `<pick>:buy:<mat key>` per AH or vendor mat (bought by the crafter), `<pick>:mail:<from>:<mat key>` per owned mat another character holds (C22), `<pick>:craft:<spell>` per intermediate, `<pick>:craft` (key = the product, `craft_plan.py:267`, as tracking matches a craft event by its recipe's product, `recommend/tracking_pass.py:23-25`; the chain's carrier), and `<pick>:post` at Claude's price.
- **The write path.** The server's connection is `mode=ro` and stays so. `McpServer.start` opens, only when `mcp_writes` is on, a second connection in read-write mode with `busy_timeout` (as `web/server.open_server_db` does for the dashboard), held by a `PickStore` (`state/picks.py`) and closed at stop or when writes are turned off. A write is one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` of one upsert, under the app's lock, after every check ran on the read-only data. The tool does **not** re-plan on its thread: it sets a request on a `ReplanRequests` queue (the twin of `__main__.RefreshRequests`, merged, consumed at the scheduler's next tick, ≤ 5 s), and the main thread runs `tracking_pass`, `replan_all` and `export_plan` on its own connection, serialized with refreshes and SavedVariables reads. Why: `replan_all` stages through the shared `*_new` tables, and two connections re-planning at once could publish a short plan (§4 contradiction 2). The tool's answer says the plan updates within seconds. Skip on the dashboard (`web/server.skip`) requests the same re-plan; the addon's skip (`state/writes.record_skip`) is already followed by a re-plan in `savedvars_changed` (`ahsync/__main__.py:283-297`).
- **A pick during a refresh.** `_stage_plan` reads the active picks inside the refresh's transaction; a pick written after that read is merged by the re-plan its own request queues. A re-plan that fails keeps the last good plan (`replan_all`'s rule), and the pick stays stored, merged at the next one. Disable closes the write connection under the lock; a write in flight answers "just turned off".

### 3.3 The merge (`engine/picks.py`, pure; called from `recommend/portfolio.plan_portfolio`)

Each rebuild, in pick-id order:

1. **Close what has ended** (`picks.transitions(picks, actions, now_ms)` → the status changes to store): a pick is `done` when its final post is `done`, or `partial` at expiry; `expired` when `now_ms ≥ expires_ms` with that post still open, or a post pick's window ended; `removed` when any of its actions is `skipped` (decision 7) or `remove_pick` closed it. A closed pick's open actions are deleted at publish (pending rows, replaced like any); its done and partial actions stay as history (§3.4.1). Stock a closed pick bought or crafted is yours: the item is open to the engine again, which sells it like any stock.
2. **Realize the active ones** (`picks.actions(pick, facts, world, now_ms, settings)` → `PlanAction`s, §3.2's shapes): the post's chance from `plan.post_chance` (`engine/plan.py:302`) at Claude's price over its window (`sellprob.posting_now_window` for a post, flip or craft; `planned_window` from the hold's end for a hold), its expected from `sellprob.expected_post` (`engine/sellprob.py:205`), its net against its cost (a flip or hold: `qty × max_buy_price`; a craft: the mats' cost). A done buy or craft keeps its post pending at Claude's price (the follow-up rule, §3.4.1, never re-priced). With no facts for the item the chance is the default (`sellprob.default_chance`). A craft's sourcing is re-run each rebuild from the current bags and book (as the engine's crafts are), with the crafter fixed to Claude's and the count to the pick's; a mat that has disappeared leaves the step in the plan at its last price with the note "no listing now" (the addon's Shopping list marks it gone, A38).
3. **Claude first** (`picks.reservations(picks)` → `OpenPosition`s): each active pick's gold needed, as an `OpenPosition(pick_id, char_id, gold)` appended to `committed`, so `accounting` lowers the remaining capacity (floored at 0) and that character's spendable gold before `select` runs (`engine/allocation.py:139-144`), and the snapshot's capacity (the addon's cap) follows. A done buy or craft is a real position by then, so its reservation drops to the undone share: no double count (CP5).
4. **No engine action on its item; its mats taken first** (decision 17): the picks' item keys (a craft's product) are added to `WeekInputs.ignore` for the run, the existing rule-`ignore` semantics (`engine/week.py:80-84`, O9: no lot, no fact, no mat), so the engine neither posts your other units of it nor buys it as a mat while the pick is active; the item's tooltip and pages are unchanged. A craft pick's mats are consumed from the craft market (`craft_plan.consume`, `engine/craft_plan.py:136`: the owned units, the vendor and the AH book's cheapest listings) and from the held units (`use_held`, line 164) before `generate`, so the engine's crafts and flips of that mat see what is left (CP16).
5. **Into the plan**: Claude's actions come first in plan order, then the engine's; `plan_rows` keeps their ids and sets `origin`.

**With the sold-price batch** (`docs/plans/sold-price.md`): SP1 changes nothing here. SP2 moves the engine's post cap and windows to the sold price; a pick keeps Claude's price, so SP2 never moves it, but its chance comes from the same `post_chance`, so it follows SP2 like every post. If SP2 lands first, check 6's band adds `sold_med7` as a reference ("below 10% of what sold"); the spec merge of whichever lands second says so.

### 3.4 Lifecycle

| Status | Set by | In the plan | Shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| `active` | `add_pick` | merged each rebuild; its gold reserved first; its item closed to the engine; a craft's mats taken first | Today / Week rows labelled Claude; `list_picks` default |
| `done` | the merge, when its final post is done (or partial at expiry) | its actions stay as history | History, per-origin totals, `list_picks status=done` with realized |
| `expired` | the merge, at `expires_ms` (a post pick: its window's end) with the post still open | open actions dropped at the next publish; bought or crafted stock stays yours and the engine sells it | `list_picks`, totals ("2 expired") |
| `removed` | Skip on any of its actions, or `remove_pick` | as expired; the engine re-plans to fill the hole; a new `add_pick` on the item is allowed | as expired, with its reason |

Per kind: a **post** is done when its post is done, expired when its window ends. A **flip** is done when its post is done; its buy done and the post open at expiry is expired (the stock is yours). A **hold** is the same, with the post dated at the hold's end; the post matches at any price from 24 h before that date to the window's end (tracking's rule, `engine/tracking.py:107-121` and `124-140`), so posting a day early when the price is met counts; earlier than that, the post is an untracked post of your own and the pick expires with the stock already sold (its position still shows the sale). A **craft** is done when its product's post is done; its mat buys, mails and crafts are steps, done or not, as the engine's chains are.

### 3.6 Security

- **Bounds**: the nine checks of §3.1; the count cap; argument sizes (a request is already ≤ 64 KB; the note ≤ 200 characters, no control characters; it is escaped like every string in `plan_lua` and drawn as text, never HTML).
- **The switch** (decision 6) is checked inside the tool under the app's lock, not in the bridge: the bridge still lists the tools (it answers `tools/list` itself), and the server refuses them when writes are off (MCP25).
- **Nothing irreversible**: a pick spends no gold; you do, in game, by your own click. Every pick is visible on both screens, labelled, and removable in one click. Turning writes off stops new picks; Disable stops everything.
- **Prompt injection**: item names come from outside, and a web page Claude reads could tell it to add picks. The switch, the caps, the labels and the gold-in-hand check bound what that can do; a pick can take the whole risk room (decision 8), which is why the card's note says so: "with this on, Claude can add trades to your plan, up to all of your risk room; it can't spend your gold".
- **The transport** is unchanged: the key, `Host` and `Origin` checks apply to every call (SEC19), and the dashboard's switch is a token-checked POST (SEC18's twin, SEC21).
- **SEC scenarios** (§5): the switch off; a pick without the key; the caps; a hostile note; the bands; a write while a refresh runs; `query` can't write `claude_picks`.
- `/security-review` and the independent review on CP3a, CP3b and CP4.

### 3.7 Non-goals

- Mail and disenchant picks; a craft pick whose product is an enchant (no product key: nothing to post).
- Changing a pick in place (remove, then add); a pick that re-prices itself; a hold that posts when the price is met (the post is dated at the hold's end; the user may post up to a day early, §3.4).
- A per-mat price ceiling from Claude (decision 16).
- An approval step (decision 3), notifications.
- Any engine formula change; the Lua engine twins; the addon writing picks; the engine-action Skip fix (decision 14).

## 9. Risks

- **Reservation and position overlap** (CP5): the window between a buy's purchase event and the position pass could count the gold twice for one rebuild. The merge computes the reservation from the action's `done_qty`, which the tracking pass sets before the re-plan, so the overlap is at most the gap between an in-game buy and the next SavedVariables read, the same as any funded flip.
- **Price checks on stale data**: the band and a craft's mat prices are against the last refresh (hourly for the cloud market). The answer names the refresh time and lists the mats, and the pick's sell price is Claude's, so a stale band only ever refuses, never mis-prices; a craft's mats are re-sourced each rebuild.
- **A craft's mats competing with the engine** (decision 17): the pick takes the cheapest listings first, so an engine craft of the same mat gets dearer ones and may stop paying. That is the point of Claude first; the engine's row then shows its exclusion reason.
- **The addon's reselection** spends within the snapshot's capacity, already lowered; but a pick added between two exports is unknown to the addon until the next `PortfolioPlan.lua`, so the addon's own crafts could briefly use Claude's room. Bounded by the re-plan and export that follow a pick within seconds.
- **Two re-plan paths**: until the rules endpoint moves to the queue (§4 contradiction 2), the dashboard's rules re-plan and the queued one can still interleave as today; no worse than now.
- **`chain_actions` with a given post price**: it is valued through `best_sale`'s `OptionValue` today (`craft_plan.py:220-221`); CP2b adds an optional argument or builds the value from Claude's price. `craft_plan.py` is not ported, so no Lua twin moves; if it proves awkward, `picks.py` builds the craft chain's `PlanAction`s itself in the same shape, which CP18 checks against tracking.
- **`craft_option` at a fixed count**: it stops where a craft no longer pays (`engine/craft.py:339-342`, "each extra craft must still pay"). Check 7 turns that into a refusal rather than a smaller pick, so the engine's rule is used as a check, not changed; a free-standing sourcing without the pay rule would mean touching the ported module.
