Proposal: Cooldowns tab, in the addon and on the dashboard

Draft 3, approved 2026-11-20; corrected in CD1 by the catalog research (Philosopher's Stone, Elemental Fire's own 10 minutes, Iron to Gold 1 d, the SnowMaster 9000). This draft folds in the independent review and the user's answers: shared transmute cooldowns, "Not read", "Yes (bank)", and the planner fix deferred to a later batch. It is a research view of items made by profession cooldowns: transmutes, Mooncloth, Refined Deeprock Salt and the like. They are worth a lot because nobody can make many of them. The tab answers which cooldown items exist for a profession, what skill each needs, how long the cooldown is, what it sells for now, what one use earns after mats and the AH cut, whether each character is ready, and whether they can make it now. All prices, skills, cooldown lengths and the transmute group below are illustrative. The real values come from the catalog (section 1) and the market.

The illustrative account has six characters with a cooldown profession:

  • Amaya (logged in): Alchemy 280 / Herbalism 290.
  • Tessa: Alchemy 300. She used Transmute: Arcanite yesterday, so the whole shared transmute cooldown has 1 d 2 h left.
  • Sorvin: Alchemy 230.
  • Brugan: Leatherworking 262 / Skinning 260, with a Salt Shaker in his bags.
  • Mira: Tailoring 260 / Leatherworking 255, no Salt Shaker.
  • Keldor: Tailoring 245.

In this illustration all Alchemy transmutes share one cooldown (the catalog group "transmute"). Whether they do on Forever is confirmed while the catalog is built. Net per use = units made × AH unit price × 0.95 (the AH cut) − mats at their AH price. The deposit is left out of this illustration only; the build uses engine/fees like every other tab.

1. How the list of cooldown items is derived

The game's client cannot tell us which recipes have a cooldown, what skill they need, or how long the cooldown is (SPEC §2.2.1 captures both as "unknown"; §6 item 7). A high AH price never makes something a cooldown item either.

  • So the list is curated by us and shipped complete inside the addon from its first release. Every addon user gets every cooldown recipe on day one, without playing or adding anything.
  • The game never builds the list. It only tells us when each character is ready, and flags anything the curated list missed. We fix the list and ship the fix to everyone with the next addon update.
1. The catalog (static file, curated)
ahsync/data/cooldown_catalog.json, generated into the addon as Data/Cooldowns.lua with the same version.

In WoW's own spell data, a craft is a cooldown craft when its spell has a recovery time: the spell's cooldown, or its category's (a shared cooldown, the catalog's group). Classic databases show this as "Cooldown: 2 days" on the spell page.

So the rule is every profession spell that creates an item and has a non-zero cooldown, plus every tool item whose Use spell creates an item and has a cooldown (the Salt Shaker). One row per spell, each with its source URL.

2. The game says when you're ready (addon)
Three sources, and the game's own report wins:
  1. the game reports the time left: C_TradeSkillUI.GetRecipeCooldown when a profession window opens, and C_Container.GetItemCooldown for a tool at login and on bag updates, with no window needed;
  2. the addon saw a catalog spell cast succeed, and records the cast time plus the catalog's period;
  3. neither has happened yet: Not read.
The APIs are UNVERIFIED on Forever; source 2 works without them. A recipe the game reports a cooldown for that the catalog lacks becomes a seen in game row.
→
3. The market only prices it
The AH price and sales come from the same place as every other tab: the intelligence's current price. A price more than 7 days old shows muted with its age, and "—" means no price was ever seen. An item is never added because it is expensive.

Cross-checks on Status:
  • a catalog item the market doesn't know (likely changed on Forever);
  • the addon's catalog version against the one AHSync exports.

2. Addon: the Cooldowns tab

A seventh tab after Craft.

  • What it lists: the chosen character's professions only, but every catalog recipe of those professions, learned or not and whatever the skill.
  • Sort: by AH unit price, highest first; ties by recipe name A–Z.
  • The character row: lists your characters that have a profession with cooldown recipes. It starts on the one you're logged in as ("you"); click another name to see theirs.
  • Nothing is recalculated for the other characters. Their skill, known recipes, ready times, bags and bank are what the addon stored when they last played (the bar says when). The money columns are what AHSync exported.
  • No money in the addon: the addon computes no money at all. Without AHSync, those columns show "—".
  • Hovers: the AH price or the item name shows the normal AHProfit market tooltip; Mats lists each mat in units (needed, held, to buy).
  • Shared cooldowns: a transmute used puts every transmute on cooldown. The Cooldown cell says "shared", and several "Yes" rows on one character are alternatives: one per cooldown.

Column rules

Character rowYour characters with a profession that has catalog (or seen-in-game) cooldown recipes: the logged-in one first, marked "you", then the rest by name. More than 6 wrap to a second line. A character without such a profession is not in the row.
Item · recipeThe product name in its quality colour; the profession when the character has two; the recipe; and "makes N" when it makes more than 1.
SkillThe catalog's required skill. Amber with "you N" (logged in) or "has N" (another character) when below it.
CooldownHow long the recipe is locked after each use: "2 d", "3 d", "20 h". "shared" when it is in a group. Hovering lists the group ("one cooldown for all 11 transmutes; Elemental Fire has its own") and says that using any of them locks all of them for the period of the one used.
Ready
  • ready, or the time left (the group's, for a shared cooldown).
  • In orange when the skill is high enough:
    • Not learned: the recipe is missing.
    • Needs item: the tool (a transmute's Philosopher's Stone, the Salt Shaker) is in neither this character's bags nor bank. Another character's copy doesn't count. When a transmute lacks both the recipe and the Stone, Not learned shows.
    • Not read: the ready time has never been captured. Hover: "Salt Shaker's cooldown hasn't been read yet. Carry it in your bags at login, or use it once." For a recipe: "Open Alchemy once, or use it."
  • "—" when the skill is missing.
  • Hovering names what's missing. A tool held only in the bank counts as held, and the hover adds "Salt Shaker is in the bank".
  • For other characters, the time counts down from the stored ready time. It is refreshed when the tab is shown and on capture events, never every frame.
AH priceThe product's current lowest unit price (the tooltip's price). Sorted by this, highest first. Muted with its age when more than 7 days old ("9 d old"). "—" when no price has ever been seen. Hovering shows the normal AHProfit market tooltip for the item.
MatsThe mats per use at their AH price, as exported. Hovering lists each mat in units only: "Arcane Crystal: need 1 · Amaya has 1 (bags) · buy 0".
Per week (dashboard)Net / use × 7 ÷ the cooldown in days. "—" when the cooldown is under 1 day (Elemental Fire's 10 m): the cooldown doesn't limit it, the mats do.
Net / useExported by AHSync: units made × price after the cut, less the deposit and the mats. Red when below zero. "—" without a price. Hovering says which export it comes from ("from the 14:32 export").
Can Make
  • Yes when this character can use it right now: the skill is met, the recipe is learned, the tool is held, the cooldown (or its group's) is ready, and the mats are in this character's own bags.
  • Yes (bank) when a needed mat or the tool is only in the bank (as of the last bank visit).
  • No otherwise. Hovering gives the first reason: "on cooldown 1 d 2 h", "missing 1 Essence of Water", "not learned", "needs Salt Shaker", "cooldown not read", "needs skill 300".
  • In a shared group, several Yes rows are alternatives: the hover says "shared: one of these per cooldown".
Also ready nowLogged-in view only: the other characters with a stored ready cooldown, counted once per group, with up to 3 recipe names each.

3. Dashboard: the Cooldowns page

A research page, desktop only, next to Craft and Gathering.

  • What it lists: every profession's cooldown recipes from the catalog. Three filters narrow it.
  • Sort: by AH unit price, highest first. Every column header sorts.
  • Built when the page loads, from the stored ready times and the current time, so every countdown is current.
  • Per week is what one character earns using this recipe every time it is ready: net per use × 7 ÷ cooldown days. For a shared group, the rows are alternatives, not a total.
  • Who lists every character with the profession, one line each, best first: Ready, then soonest; then not learned, needs item or not read (orange); then below the skill. Ties are by name.

Can Make column
  • Yes when at least one of your characters can use it right now. This is the addon's rule: skill, learned, tool, cooldown or group ready, and the mats in that character's own bags. Hovering lists who: "Sorvin (bags)". Gold Bar is Yes through Sorvin; Amaya is ready but holds no Iron Bar.
  • Yes (bank) when the only way is with a mat or the tool from the bank. Truesilver Bar: Amaya's Mithril Bar is in her bank, and Sorvin has none.
  • No otherwise. Hovering gives each character's reason. Mats on another character don't count: they would have to be mailed first.
  • Clicking the header sorts Yes, then Yes (bank), then No; ties by AH price.
Who column rules
  • One line per character with the profession, best first: "<name> Ready", then the soonest time left, then "<name> not learned", "<name> needs item" or "<name> not read" (orange; hovering names what's missing), then "<name> needs N (has M)". Ties are by name.
  • A shared cooldown shows the group's time on every row of the group. Tessa's 1 d 2 h is on every transmute she knows.
  • More than 3 characters: "N Ready of M", the first 3 lines, then "+K more" with a breakdown, and the full list on hover.
  • "—" when none of your characters has the profession.
  • Per week is per character (D4). The column does not add characters, because one market can rarely absorb several characters' output.
Status page additions
  • "cooldown catalog: 15 recipes", or "cooldown catalog: 1 row skipped (Mooncloth: skill 350 outside 1–300)".
  • "Seen in game, not in the catalog: Transmute: Starsilver (Amaya). Add it to cooldown_catalog.json."
  • "Catalog item unknown to the market: 12360 Arcanite Bar" (Forever may have changed it).
  • "cooldown catalog v2 in the addon, v1 in AHSync's export: update AHSync" (or the other way round).

4. Not in this proposal

  • The planner fix (D3). Wanted, but deferred to a later batch, after the planning algorithm is reworked following the 2026-11-21 review. Until then the Craft and Portfolio plans don't use the catalog's cooldowns.
  • Not built this time:
    • reminders or alerts when a cooldown is ready;
    • the character dropdown (the row wraps instead);
    • the report tool for seen-in-game recipes;
    • the cooldown-length and mats cross-checks.
  • Choosing which transmute to spend in a shared group: the tab shows them side by side; the higher Net / use is the answer.
  • Buying the mats: the tab is research only.
