A. A plain name: no link (recommended now)

The name keeps its quality colour and loses the underline and the click. Nothing leaves the app. The Item column's header hover changes from "…Click to open it on auctionhouse.fyi" to "The item, in its quality colour". Smallest change, and nothing misleading is left behind.

B. A link to the item's own page on another site

The name opens that item's page, by item id. Needs a site with a stable address per item, and I haven't checked that any of them works for Forever's items: AHledger's per-item pages are unverified, and a database site such as Wowhead Classic (/classic/item=<id>) shows Classic data, not Forever's market. A suffix item (Grunt's Bracers of Defense) would open its base item. Shown with a placeholder site name.

Hovering a name shows Open Copper Chain Belt on <site>.

C. Click the row: the item detail opens under the table (recommended next)

As on the pages where you can already select a row (Inventory, Gathering): the whole row is clickable and the selected row gets the gold edge. The detail below it is the approved item detail (item-detail-lines.html, D105), whose Market lines include the Disenchant Value breakdown per mat, so you can see why a row's Expected Value is what it is. The name is plain, as in A. The detail's values here are an example made to match the row (47c from 1.2 dust at 25c and 0.3 essence at 57c).

D. Separate follow-up: the table scrolls inside its own box

Not about links. On Windows the sideways scrollbar sits under the last row, so you can't see it (Shift + wheel works). Here the table is its own scroll box, as tall as the window (300 px in this mockup), so its sideways scrollbar is always at the bottom of the screen and the header row stays put while you scroll down. The Chance cell's "volume unknown" stays on one line, so rows are two lines tall instead of three. It would apply to every long table. Try scrolling it.
