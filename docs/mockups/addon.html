AHProfit (addon): UI mockup

This defines the look of the in-game addon (SPEC §4.3). Sizes are in game pixels at UI scale 1, and names and numbers are placeholders (your characters hold 31g 40s in total, which is what risk % is measured against). The real UI uses Blizzard fonts and textures, so the expected match is layout, content, colours and states, not exact pixels. Click the tabs to see each view. The desktop dashboard is in dashboard.html.

What each tooltip field means

Current AH priceThe cheapest listing on the AH right now, and how long ago that price was seen.
Historical priceThe median price over the last 30 days, and how much higher or lower that is than the current AH price.
Disenchant valueWhat disenchanting one is worth, and how much higher or lower that is than the current AH price. Left out when none of your characters can disenchant it.
Best time to sellThe item's best selling window in the week: the hours where price × number of buyers is highest, learned from past weeks. Shows "(early pattern)" until there are 3 weeks of data.
Price at best timeThe expected price in that window, and how much higher (or lower) that is than the current AH price.
Chance to sellHow likely a normal-size post made now sells within the next 8 hours.
