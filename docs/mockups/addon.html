AHProfit (addon): UI mockup

This defines the look of the in-game addon (SPEC §4.3). Sizes are in game pixels at UI scale 1, and names and numbers are placeholders (your characters hold 31g 40s in total, which is what risk % is measured against). The real UI uses Blizzard fonts and textures, so the expected match is layout, content, colours and states, not exact pixels. Click the tabs to see each view. The Portfolio tab replaces the old Today and Week tabs (the dashboard keeps both pages); it scrolls, and an unclipped copy is under States. The desktop dashboard is in dashboard.html.

2. AH side panel (docked right of the auction house, 300 wide)

Clicking a Shopping row searches for that item. Clicking a Post row fills the AH's Sell tab with the item, quantity and list price; you check it and press the AH's own Post button (A29). Right-click any row to keep the item for yourself. Both lists show funded rows first, then unfunded ones, muted with an “unfunded” tag: they're still clickable, for when you choose to go past your risk tolerance. Shopping lists only what to buy on the AH: a mat the plan buys from a vendor stays on the Portfolio tab's Today section, as a line under its craft marked “from a vendor” (A32, A71). ✓ found at or below target · ✗ gone or no longer profitable · … still being searched. For says why it's bought: CRAFT, FLIP or DE (A40). A search that lists nothing marks the row ✗ gone (A38); buyouts lower Qty, and a row drops off once it's all bought (A39). The bar under the status line follows any full scan: waiting, reading n of total, saving, then “full scan done” (A43). Covers UI7.

3. Tooltip and right-click menu

Since M36 the tooltip's lines are section 3b's (Market Price, Price history, Market Inventory, Best Buy / Best Sell Price, the Disenchant breakdown, Inventory; no Activity line). The variants below still show the Do line's cases (unchanged) and the right-click menu; their market lines are the M13 layout.

Hovering an item adds an AHProfit block under the item's own lines (UI8). It is drawn with two-column lines like GameTooltip:AddDoubleLine, label left and value right, at most 320 wide. All prices are per unit; differences are green above the current price and red below. The first line after the header is Do: what to do with this item, with the verb in its badge colour. The variants below cover every case. Lines that would pass 320 are shortened: hour ranges become "Sat 17–21", and a long Do value continues on a second line with an empty label (variants 1, 2, 4, 8, 9 and 14). A Best-time line is never dropped: when now is the best time it says "now" (variants 9 and 14). Right-clicking any row in the window or the AH panel opens the keep menu (SPEC §3.4.3, A25): keep all of the item for this character, keep at least a number of units (only what you hold above it is suggested), or sell normally again. SPEC GAP: the blacklist rules (§3.4.2) are not in the addon's menu yet.

What each tooltip line means

AHProfit · data ageThe header, with how old MarketIntelligence.lua is. The age and its dot turn amber past 24 h.
DoThe plan's action for the character you're on (PortfolioPlan.lua); without one, the addon's own best option. The verb has its badge colour: POST gold, BUY blue, CRAFT orange, DE purple, VENDOR and HOLD grey, MAIL light blue, KEPT and UNFUNDED muted. The value is what the action makes, then its chance; the chance belongs to the action (for a craft, its product's). A second line carries what doesn't fit: the next step or a reason. An AH listing that is on none of your lists has no Do line.
MarketThe lowest price now with its age ("now" while you browse the AH, else how old the scan is), then the 30-day median and its difference from the price now.
DisenchantWhat disenchanting one is worth and its difference from the price now. Only shown when one of your characters can disenchant it.
Best time to sell / buyThe week's top window, its expected price, the difference from now and its strength: early (under 3 weeks of data), pattern (3–5 weeks) or established (6 weeks or more). Never left out. When now falls inside the best sell window it reads "now (Sat 17–21) · 12s 30c · pattern", and the Do line adds "best time to sell is now" (on a second value-only line if it doesn't fit; variant 14). When the price now is below every buy window's expected price it reads "now · cheaper than Tue 20–23 (7s 10c)", shortened to "now · under Tue 20–23 (7s 10c)" when it would pass 320 wide (variant 9). On an AH browse row the Do line then adds "best time to buy is now".
ActivitySales per day, units listed now and days of supply, in muted text.
No market dataThe header with the data age, and "no market data yet"; then Disenchant Value when it would show (a bound item has no market row, TT44).

3b. Tooltip layout (approved 2026-11-07, built M36)

Built (M33b-2, 2026-11-08), approved 2026-11-07: the market lines take one shape (title, qualifier on the right, values under it), Sales Rate becomes Sell Chance Now and Disenchant becomes Disenchant Value: see sell-through.html, which supersedes the variants below once M33b is built. Since 2026-11-08 (TT42) the layout is tooltip-layout.html's variant C: values next to their titles, coloured section titles, divider lines.

Order: Do, Market Price, Price history, Market Inventory, Sales Rate, Best Buy Price, Best Sell Price, Disenchant with its mats (gear only), Inventory. Market Price: the lowest listing at the newest observation with its age ("now" at the AH), then how it compares with the 7-day median: "11% lower than 7d median" (red), "12% higher than 7d median" (green), "at 7d median" when the difference rounds to 0%; when the line would pass 320 wide the comparison continues on its own line (variant 1); (price − 7d median) ÷ 7d median, whole percent half up; left out when there is no 7-day median. Price history: the 30, 14 and 7-day medians, each after its span in brackets ("(30d) 55c"), oldest to newest so a rise reads left to right, then the shift: rising when the 7-day median is at least 10% above the 30-day one, falling at least 10% below, else steady; until 30 days of data exist the missing medians read "—" and no word is shown. Price history is a title line with the word on its right, and the medians on the line under it, so the line fits 320 wide even with gold prices. Market Inventory: units listed now. Sales Rate: units sold per day (the 7-day estimate, §3.3.6); while fewer than 7 of the last 7 days have sales data, the count follows, muted ("55 sold/day · 2 days of data", variant 1; M40, TT32); "— sold/day" when the sales are unknown, "0 sold/day" when nothing sold. Days of supply went in M40. Disenchant (gear you or an alt can disenchant): a title line with how the total compares with the AH price on its right ("69% lower than AH price", red below, green above), then one line per possible mat: its chance, its average quantity (the middle of its range: 1–2 is 1.5, 2–5 is 3.5; shown with one decimal only when it has one) and latest price, and its share, chance × average quantity × price; then a sum line with no label: the total, the expected value of one disenchant, on the right under the shares, which add up to it (rounded only for display). A mat with no price reads "no price" in amber and adds nothing. These are latest prices before the AH cut; the plan's disenchant decision uses expected sale values, so a Do: DISENCHANT net can be lower. Best Buy / Best Sell Price: the expected price at the best window and its day and time in words (Morning 6–12, Afternoon 12–18, Evening 18–23, Night 23–6, a night after midnight named by the evening before), shown from the first data, followed by the window's strength: early (in orange: under 3 weeks of data at that hour of the week), pattern (3–5 weeks) or established (6 or more); the cloud analytics (M33/M34) will sharpen them. When a line would pass 320 wide the strength continues on its own line (variant 3). Inventory: what you hold across your characters, then each place: "Total: 29 · Bags: 4 · Bank: 1 · Mail: 24", places with none left out (M40, TT30). The Activity line is dropped; its sales per day move to Sales Rate.

5. Minimap button

A round button on the minimap's rim with the AHSync logo (the app icon's gold coin, cropped round), so the window is one click away without typing /ahp. Left- or right-click opens or closes the window, as /ahp does. Drag moves it around the rim; the position is kept for the account (AHProfitDB.minimap.angle, default 225°, bottom left). Hover shows the data age and how many of the plan's actions are due now on this character, with their expected net, from the same view model as the window (no arithmetic in frame code). An amber dot appears when the window would show a problem: data over 24 h old, no market file, or files from another AHSync version. /ahp minimap hides or shows it. No library (no LibDBIcon); in combat a click opens nothing, as /ahp doesn't.
