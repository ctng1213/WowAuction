3. What each line means

Coverage (header)The user, 2026-11-07: "data 12 min old · 5% coverage". The hours the market was observed in the longest lookback the lines use, 30 days, ÷ 720: house 114 has about 33 observed hours, so 5%. A missed hour counts against it; it reaches 100% after 30 full days. It describes the market's data; the best times keep their own strength word, which describes the item's pattern (a rarely listed item can stay early under 100% coverage). The market file carries it (from the summary's observed hours); "—" for a market without them.
The qualifier wordOne rule for Market Price and Market Inventory (Sell Chance has none: its value now says it), from the value now against its 7, 14 and 30 days: rising when now > 7d > 14d > 30d, each step at least 5% up (consistently growing); falling the mirror; otherwise, in flux, against 7d: high at 10% or more above, low at 10% or more below, else steady. (Market Price today says rising/falling when 7d is 10% off 30d; this replaces it, so the lines agree: decided by the user, 2026-11-07, with "steady" as the word.) Linen's inventory: 24,808 now against 14,859 in every window is not a climb, so it reads high; the gloves' 1,713 against 2,751 reads low.
Market PriceRight: the lowest listing now (or its age, "19c (2 h)") and the qualifier word. Values: the medians of the hourly lowest listing over 7, 14 and 30 days. Price history folds into it (its title line and values are this line now).
Market InventoryA stock, not a flow: units on the AH at a moment, never units sold. Right: units listed in the newest snapshot ("listed now") and the qualifier word. Values: the typical units listed over 7, 14 and 30 days (the median of each day's median listed units, as median_daily_supply computes for 30 days today).
Sell Chance NowAlways for a 24 h post, the usual auction length (the user, 2026-11-07: the line doesn't say it; the definition and the hover do). Right: now, the forecast that a 24 h auction posted now at the current market price (the lowest listing, here 19c) sells within 24 h: the engine's posting-now simulation, run over 24 h instead of 8, with today's competing listings, the measured undercutters and the measured sales; no qualifier word. The now values drawn (74%, 5%) are illustrative; the history values are measured. Values: over 7, 14 and 30 days, the measured share of listings posted at the lowest price that were bought within 24 h: the share that left early (gone while it could not have expired) times the share of early leaves that were sales, not cancels. For commodities the cancels are measured (a listing that vanished while a cheaper one stayed untouched was not bought; Forever's commodity buys take the cheapest units first, 4,574 of 4,583 fills). For gear no rule tells a cancel from a sale, so the cancel share is estimated (the commodity market's measured share until your own posts calibrate it) and the line says est.: gloves 7% left early × (1 − 11% cancels) ≈ 6%. Under 30 listings in a window it borrows from the item's class and says "class est."; with nothing to borrow, "—".
Best Sell TimeRenamed from Best Sell Price (the user, 2026-11-07). The top selling window's expected price and its best hour, as built, then a percentage: the chance a 24 h auction posted for that window at that price sells before it expires (the Sell Chance Now model, run for that hour and price), in the same shape as the other lines (the user, 2026-11-07): the title with its strength on the right (early in orange under 3 weeks of that window seen for this item, pattern 3–5, established 6+), and on the line under, the price, the time and the percentage. Gear says est. as Sell Chance does.
Best Buy TimeRenamed from Best Buy Price. The top buying window's expected price and its best hour, as built, then a percentage: the chance of finding it at or below that price then, the share of recent weeks whose lowest listing in that window was at or below it (recency-weighted like the windows, half-life 2 weeks); "—" before a week of data.
Disenchant ValueRenamed from Disenchant (the user, 2026-11-07), in the shared shape: the title with, on its right, the expected value of one disenchant at the mats' current AH prices and its difference from the market price, "37% below AH · 63c" (the comparison first, red below, green above; (63 − 100) ÷ 100, then the value); on the lines under, one per possible mat, "80% · 1.5 Strange Dust @ 25c" and its share 30c, as built (TT29). The separate total line goes: the total is on the title line.
Do (unchanged)The same model, for your units at the post's price within the post's window (8 h now, or the selling window). Sell Chance now is that model for one listing at the current market price over 24 h, so the two can differ.

Not drawn, to keep the change small: the same three blocks on the dashboard's item detail (as lines, the same values), and Sell Chance at other price tiers (within 5%, within 15%) on the dashboard only.
