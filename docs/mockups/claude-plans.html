Proposal: Claude's picks in the plan

Approved by the user 2026-11-10 (plan docs/plans/claude-plans.md §1, CP0–CP5; the user's decisions in docs/plans/mcp-server.md §11). Claude may add its own trades to your plan: a pick is a plan action like the engine's, labelled Claude, shown and tracked the same way. Four kinds: a post of your stock, a flip, a craft, and a hold (buy, then post at a target price when the hold ends). Its gold is reserved ahead of the engine, which fills only the room left and plans nothing on that item; a pick may take all of your risk room (the user, 2026-11-10), and Claude is told so. Three things change on screen, each marked new below: a second switch on the AI assistant card (decided: option B), the Claude label on plan rows with a per-origin line in the footers, and a per-origin line in History's tile. Numbers are the mockups' world (Amaya 21 g, Brugan 10 g 40 s, capacity 6 g 49 s 40 c); the picks are a flip of 40 Mageweave Cloth (buy at or below 8 s, sell at 12 s) and a craft of 2 Silver Rod by Brugan (sell at 75 s, mats 2 Silver Bar from the AH at 30 s).

With picks on, what Claude reads on the web can shape the trades it adds, up to all of your risk room; every pick is labelled, needs your own click in game to spend anything, and one Skip removes it.

5. What this mockup does not change

The Today and Week lists' columns, sorting and footers; the addon's other tabs, the tooltip and the AH panel; every page without a pick (the fixture world has none, so every golden but the new UI26 stays). A pick's row behaves as its kind does: its buy is on the Shopping list, its post fills the Sell tab, a done buy opens a position on the Portfolio tab. The engine's rows after a pick are whatever the optimizer funds with the capacity left; this mockup shows one outcome of that.
