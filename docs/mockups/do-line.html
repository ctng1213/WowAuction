Proposal: what the Do line's money means

Round 3, 2026-11-13. Decided so far: one shape for your stock and bought stock; Sells for (what the units bring in if they sell, after the AH's 5%) with the chance; Paid only when the plan bought the stock; no "then nothing"; now, or later with its time frame; the quantity as 20× (also on CRAFT and MAIL: "4× Copper Rod", "MAIL 6× to Brugan"); when on the Do line and quantity and price under it, for now and later alike. New: the money on line 2 has two modes, switched in game and kept for the account, like /ahp minimap:

full default/ahp profit full: the profit if it all sells, Profit = Sells for − Paid, and the chance is told separately on line 1. Your own stock shows no line 2: its profit would equal Sells for.
expected/ahp profit expected: the plan's Expected Profit, which averages in the chance of not selling (the unsold units go to the vendor or disenchanting, and the deposit is lost), less Paid. Shown for your own stock too.
Either/ahp profit alone says which mode is on. The setting changes only the wording and the number shown; the plan, its ranking and what it funds are the same in both.

4. Notes on building it

Sells for, Profitqty × price × 0.95, and Sells for − Paid, made in UI/ViewModel.lua and tested.
PaidOne new field: AHSync adds each action's cost basis to PortfolioPlan.lua (it already computes it, SPEC §3.4 expected net). Until a file has it, every post shows the your-stock shape.
The settingAccount-wide, in AHProfitDB, like the minimap button's (UI23); read at draw time, so a switch shows on the next hover without a /reload.
Time frameFrom the post time to the selling window's best hour; both are in the plan file already.
LayoutLine 1: Do: POST now or Do: POST Sat 10:00–18:00, Sells for on the right. Line 2, indented: quantity and price ("20× at 18c"; a post later adds "· +53% vs now"), Paid and Profit on the right. A post later from bought stock puts Paid and Profit on a third line, since its line 2 is full. Every line fits 320 px at the small font; anything longer moves under, as today (TT31).
